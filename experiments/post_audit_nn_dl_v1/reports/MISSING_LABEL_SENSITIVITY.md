# Missing-label sensitivity

**Status: COMPLETED.** No missing label is recoded to zero in the primary evaluation.

The frozen ex-ante universe contains 62,797 forecast candidates, of which 62,238 have observed outcomes and 559 do not. Among 41,131 ex-ante inactive-entry candidates, 40,572 outcomes are known, 559 remain unknown, and 1,458 observed entries occurred.

Observed entry prevalence among labeled candidates is 3.594%. The assumption-free binary prevalence interval is [3.545%, 4.904%] if every unknown is respectively a failure or a success.

The table provides annual observed-versus-missing covariate composition, full-population Top-10 entry-rate bounds for every model-seed-pool, capped-gain NDCG bounds, and a forecast-error stress interval. The latter caps an unknown outcome at the historical training maximum for its HS2 chapter; it is an empirical stress scenario, not a guaranteed upper bound. Without a defensible finite outcome cap, full-population USD MAE contrasts are not finitely identified.
