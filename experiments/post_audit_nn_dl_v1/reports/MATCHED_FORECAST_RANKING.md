# Matched forecasting versus market selection

Forecast MAE and ranking NDCG are recomputed on identical known-label candidate sets for each decision task. New-market-entry and existing-underexploited populations are kept separate. Lower USD MAE is better; higher task-specific NDCG is better. The across-model Spearman coefficient is descriptive over the seven comparable prediction strategies, not an inferential result.
