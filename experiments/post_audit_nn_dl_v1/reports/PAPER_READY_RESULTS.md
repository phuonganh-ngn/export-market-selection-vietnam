# Paper-ready empirical results (post-audit neural extension)

## Data

The analysis uses Vietnam bilateral HS12 BACI V202601 trade aggregated to HS2 and the existing audited destination–product panel. The 2022–2024 primary evaluation universe contains 62,797 ex-ante eligible destination–HS2–year observations; 62,238 outcome labels are observed and 559 are unobserved. Unknown trade outcomes are retained as missing rather than coded as zero. The available macroeconomic and governance observations are retrospective current vintages.

## Research design and empirical strategy

The primary protocol imposes a two-year information lag and chronological outer folds for 2022, 2023, and 2024. Hyperparameter selection, preprocessing, categorical encoding, imputation, scaling, and regression calibration use only earlier training/validation data. Five fixed training seeds are used for stochastic MLP/TabM fits. The MLP is a feed-forward multilayer network; TabM uses the official parameter-efficient ensemble implementation (k=8). Baselines include persistence, PPML, and saved tree models.

The discovery target is a verified previously inactive relationship reaching USD 50,000 in the outcome year. Ranking separates new entry from existing underexploited-market expansion. Forecast accuracy uses USD MAE as the primary metric and reports log1p metrics separately. Ranking metrics are macro-averaged within product-year candidate pools; paired comparisons use the same pools.
