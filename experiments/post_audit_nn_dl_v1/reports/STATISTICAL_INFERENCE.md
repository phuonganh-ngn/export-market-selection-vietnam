# Statistical inference

All reported comparisons are paired on the same observed-label units or candidate pools. Model seeds are averaged within an economic unit and are not treated as independent observations.

Whole HS2 chapters are resampled with all years retained; a paired chapter sign-flip test supplies raw p-values, followed by Holm adjustment. There are only three outer years, so these intervals do not estimate temporal generalization.

## E3 ranking

The primary HS2-block bootstrap resamples chapters and retains the year pools; a destination-block bootstrap sensitivity resamples destinations jointly across all product-year pools and recomputes rankings. Both are cross-sectional procedures and neither resolves uncertainty across three years.

## Dependence and interpretation

HS2 bootstrap treats HS2 chapters as independent blocks, while destination bootstrap treats destinations as blocks. Both leave residual dependence across years and shared trade shocks; the three evaluation years are too few for reliable year-cluster inference. Destination-level Wilcoxon p-values that numerically underflow to zero are conservatively floored at 1e-16 before Holm adjustment; p-values below 0.0001 are displayed as <0.0001. P-values are finite-sample resampling/sign-rank evidence under these assumptions, not proof of economic importance or out-of-time stability.
