# Literature gap and prior art audit

**Review date:** 12 October 2026. This is a targeted audit of the required references and representative export-market selection work, not an exhaustive systematic review. The review does not establish absence of prior work.

## Novelty gate

The implemented PR-EMS label denotes a **standard finite-scenario minimax-regret application**, not a new robust-optimization algorithm. Robust optimization for multi-attribute alternatives with imprecise weights and ratings, including worst-case absolute and relative regret, was already developed by Wang. Weber's 2025 method explicitly chooses a relatively robust multicriteria decision using a worst-case ratio to each preference-specific optimum. Standard minimax regret is older still. PR-EMS adds a particular empirical decision specification for Vietnam product-market portfolios: two task-specific objectives, fixed cardinality, historically restricted forecast scenarios, and an explicit separation between decision uncertainty and retrospective missing-label identification. These are adaptations and an empirical design, not an established theorem or algorithmic novelty.

**Classification:**

- Finite-scenario minimax regret: **EXISTING**.
- Partial preference weights / multicriteria robustness: **EXISTING**.
- Cross-product top-K export-market application with observed trade outcomes: **EXISTING BUT ADAPTED** as an empirical setting; related export-market-selection MCDM case studies already exist.
- Joint preference and model-disagreement scenarios: **EXISTING BUT ADAPTED**; it is a Cartesian finite uncertainty set and inherits the conservatism and dependence assumptions of scenario robust optimization.
- Missing-outcome sensitivity separated from policy risk: **EXISTING BUT ADAPTED** from partial identification and offline-evaluation principles.
- A distinct PR-EMS algorithm: **UNVERIFIED NOVELTY** and not claimed here.
- New theory: none claimed. The computational checks verify the finite MILP formulation; they do not create a new theoretical result.

The first required reference details were checked on publisher pages. Wang's publisher abstract explicitly describes imprecise weights and ratings and absolute/relative regret; Weber's publisher page describes the relative performance-ratio objective; Elmachtoub and Grigas describe SPO loss; and the CEPII release page identifies HS12 V202601 as 2012–2024. Cañamares et al. discuss offline ranking evaluation choices and the consequences of unobserved entries. The AHP/TOPSIS export studies establish that export-market selection itself is not a new MCDM application.

## Implications for this extension

The defensible study is an **empirical application and evaluation framework**. It may contribute new comparative evidence about how standard robust-selection criteria behave across Vietnamese HS2 destination pools when historical model disagreement and outcome observability are made explicit. That empirical contribution remains exploratory because the old test years have already informed model development. No claim is made that this design is the first combination of robust optimization and international market selection, or that the acronym identifies a new method.
