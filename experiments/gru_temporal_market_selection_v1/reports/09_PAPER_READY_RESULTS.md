# Paper-ready results subsection (exploratory)

We evaluate a lightweight recurrent classifier on Vietnam's strict-zero Task-A candidate universe, using five annual feature vintages ending two years before the outcome. The sequence model is compared with stored out-of-time Gravity–PPML, LightGBM, and rank-fusion predictions and with a newly fitted LightGBM receiving the same lagged inputs. All 2022–2024 estimates are exploratory because those years informed earlier stages of model development.

The complete-case pool-level results are reported below. NDCG is averaged over product-year pools with at least one observed positive event; qualified USD and entry counts use the same observed-label candidate intersection.
