# Temporal ablation

The five-year GRU is the primary specification. A three-year window and leave-one-feature-group-out GRUs are prespecified sensitivities. The matched LightGBM uses the same sequences as flattened lag features; a summary-enhanced LightGBM uses deterministic training-period summaries of those same lags. Reverse-order predictions are an artificial model diagnostic, not an alternative empirical sample.
