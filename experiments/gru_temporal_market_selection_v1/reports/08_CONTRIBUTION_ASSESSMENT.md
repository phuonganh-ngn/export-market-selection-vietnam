# Contribution assessment

This extension tests whether sequential modeling adds value beyond strong M1 baselines. A standard GRU is not a methodological innovation. Its potential contribution is empirical evidence about whether recurrent learning extracts incremental information from annual trade-demand and macroeconomic histories in a sparse market-entry task.

A GRU gain over original LightGBM alone is insufficient to establish recurrent-model value. The key comparison is the lag-matched LightGBM. If that model matches or exceeds GRU, richer historical input rather than recurrent architecture is the more cautious interpretation. Ranking gains without qualified-USD gains are a ranking/economic-value divergence.

All years 2022–2024 were inspected during prior M1 development; therefore no result here is independently validated. Historical WDI/WGI revisions and 556 unobserved Task-A outcomes are additional limits.
