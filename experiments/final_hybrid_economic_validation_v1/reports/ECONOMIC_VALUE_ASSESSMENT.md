# Economic value assessment

Economic comparisons sum observed trade value or positive incremental exports among known-label candidates ranked within each pool. Separately, the full ex-ante Top-10 amount is reported only as an observed partial sum with the number of unknown outcomes; it is not treated as a complete total. These are retrospective trade outcomes, not causal gains, profit, or treatment effects.

F2 reports complete-case USD opportunity at Top-5/Top-10, raw paired pool differences, medians and quartiles, the share of pools each policy wins, USD regret against the observed-label hindsight oracle, and a 99th-percentile cap estimated only from OOF development outcomes. The cap is a tail-sensitivity diagnostic and does not estimate exporter profitability.
