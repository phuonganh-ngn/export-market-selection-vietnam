# Paper-ready empirical results

## Data

CEPII BACI HS12 V202601 trade flows are aggregated from HS6 to HS4 and HS2. The working panel contains 224 foreign destinations and 96 eligible HS2 chapters over 2014–2024. World import demand is aggregated across all exporters. The primary outcome is current-USD Vietnam exports; zeros are assigned only in cells with positive destination–product imports. WDI is retrieved through the World Bank API, WGI uses the 2026 revision, and bilateral distance and time-invariant geographic controls come from CEPII GeoDist.

## Research design and empirical strategy

Outer evaluation years are 2022, 2023, and 2024. The main specification uses annual predictors from two years before the outcome and limits fitting outcomes to the same information cutoff. A one-year retrospective lag is treated as sensitivity analysis. Hyperparameter selection uses inner chronological validation, and imputation/scaling is fit on each training split. PPML estimates a log-link conditional mean without an exporter fixed effect because the exporter is Vietnam only. Tree models use the same observed macroeconomic, trade, geography, governance, digitalization, destination-identity, and HS2 information.

## Limitations

BACI absence is not a comprehensive reported zero; the sample uses positive historical and outcome-year world-import coverage. WDI/WGI historical values are revised vintages and annual release availability is not reconstructed. Three outer years offer limited temporal inference. Destination–product observations are dependent. The findings are retrospective country-product forecasts, not firm-level market-entry advice or causal estimates of exporter gains.
