# Methodology and implementation notes

The data workflow streams yearly BACI CSVs from the release archive, aggregates HS6 records to HS4/HS2, converts `v` from thousand USD to USD, and retains validation totals. WDI time series are retrieved through the World Bank API; WGI is taken from the 2026 release; CEPII GeoDist supplies bilateral distance and geographic controls.

The gravity baseline is a log-link Poisson pseudo-maximum-likelihood conditional mean with nonnegative export values, destination and HS2 indicators, and common observed covariates. A fixed L2 penalty (`alpha = 0.1`) stabilizes the many destination/product effects and zero-flow cells; its convergence diagnostics are saved. The penalty was increased from 0.01 after the initial fit hit its iteration limit; a 2022 training-origin check converged in 986 iterations under this setting. This is a regularized PPML specification and is identified as such in the results. Random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM use the same observed covariate information and chronological boundaries. Tree models use log1p exports, but predictions are inverse-transformed and then multiplied by a scale selected from the inner chronological validation sample to minimize original-USD MAE. This replaces mean Duan smearing, which proved unstable under the highly skewed and heteroskedastic export residuals. Model tuning is bounded and uses only inner chronological validation. Results across seeds are retained.

Numerical SHAP and ALE outputs, feature ablations, destination-block uncertainty intervals, and market segments are saved as tables. No figures are produced. Where a metric is undefined or coverage is insufficient, it is left NA and the reason is documented.
